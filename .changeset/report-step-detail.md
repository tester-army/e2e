Report steps record what they were given and where they acted. `argument` holds the text a fill typed, the key a press sent, the option selected, the files uploaded, or the value an assertion expected, redacted and capped at 1024 bytes. A secret is always `<secret:name>`; other text or a key is `<withheld>` when it went into a secure field, into a node that is not itself the control taking it, or when the node could not be read. `target` holds the box of the node a step resolved to, in CSS pixels, and the point of a positioned tap; an agent step records its last action's.
