A recording's starting and ending screens are compared as routes, not URLs. A location is reduced to its pathname with every segment that looks minted per record abstracted: uuids, hex and digit runs, long tokens mixing letters and digits, text with spaces. The query, the fragment, and a trailing slash are ignored; an app that routes in the fragment (`/#/companies/1`) routes by that path. A value the call marked with `unique()` is recognized in the slug an app derives for a record path, next to its percent- and form-encoded spellings. Where two locations still differ in one segment, a slug of a record the runner never saw, the screen may settle it: at the start, the controls of the start screen recorded with the trace have to be mostly on screen again; at the end, the recorded anchors have to be. The replay policy version changes, so existing caches start over.
