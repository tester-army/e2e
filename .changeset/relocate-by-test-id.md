Replay identifies a recorded control by the strongest identity it recorded, degrading from there: its test id first, then an authored element id on web (an id ending in a digit, `mat-input-2`, or reading like a hash is a framework counter and is skipped). A control recorded with an id is found whatever its label reads now (`Like (0 likes)` that became `Unlike (1 like)`); twins resolve by their recorded position and never by label, and any rung that finds another number of them than were recorded hands off. A control without an id, or whose id is gone, is matched by its label, exactly or by shape: tallies and relative times fold (`Reply (0 replies)` finds `Reply (1 reply)`, `Bob · now` finds `Bob · 5m`), while a number that names something does not (`Page 2` never finds `Page 3`). End anchors compare their labels as they read apart from relative times, so a recorded `Count: 1` does not pass at `Count: 0`; they are the leaves that appeared ranked by durability, and skip relative times and social tallies. An `end-mismatch` hand-off names the anchors the screen did not show in `step.cache.missingAnchors` and in the agent's notice. The replay policy version is bumped, so existing recordings re-record on their next passing run.
