New `@e2e-dev/testmu` package: `mobile({ device: testmu({ device, osVersion, app }) })` runs a mobile target on TestMu AI (formerly LambdaTest) Android emulators, iOS simulators, and real devices, one per worker slot, with `LT_USERNAME` and `LT_ACCESS_KEY` from the run's environment. An attempt that records video links TestMu AI's recording of its session.
