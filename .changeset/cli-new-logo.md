`e2e init` and `e2e --help` show the new e2e logo, its six rows of bars drawn in full blocks, one row per bar. In `e2e init` it is drawn row by row from the top like a scan in under half a second. The logo needs a terminal at least 52 columns wide, and prints without motion on one shorter than 7 rows.
